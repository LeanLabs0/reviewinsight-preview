What the score measures

Full method

A score is what reviewers rate a product, moved toward the middle when the evidence behind that rating is thin, stale or contradictory. These four decide how far it moves.

Recentweight 30%

How much of the evidence is from the last year, and how fresh the newest review is.

Reliableweight 25%

Whether the sites agree, and whether written reviews match the stars they carry.

Results-specificweight 25%

How often reviewers name an outcome you could actually check.

Resonanceweight 20%

Whether reviewers recommend the product or merely put up with it.

Where the sites disagree most

The gap between a vendor's highest and lowest rated site, in stars. Every vendor has one. A wide gap means no single rating tells the story.
