Method

What the score measures

Four things decide how far a vendor moves away from neutral. Every one of them is published, and every number on a vendor page can be rebuilt by hand.

Recentweight 30%

How much of the evidence comes from the last year, and how fresh the newest review is.

Reliableweight 25%

Whether the sites agree with each other, and whether written reviews match the stars they carry.

Results-specificweight 25%

How often reviewers name an outcome a stranger could check, rather than saying it saves time.

Resonanceweight 20%

Whether reviewers recommend the product or merely put up with it.
